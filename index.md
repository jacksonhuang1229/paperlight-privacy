# 《紙光》隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 17 日

《紙光》（套件名稱：com.paperlight.puzzle）是一款可完全離線遊玩的遊戲。

## 資料處理

我們不透過本遊戲收集或傳送玩家的個人資料。本遊戲沒有廣告，亦未整合使用者行為分析或當機回報 SDK。

本遊戲不提供應用程式內購、帳號登入或雲端存檔，也不存取照片、位置或聯絡人等資料。

## 本機資料的保存與刪除

遊戲進度與設定僅儲存在您的手機上，不會由本遊戲上傳至開發者的伺服器。

您可透過 Android 系統設定清除本應用程式的儲存資料，以刪除本機遊戲進度與設定。清除資料後，遊戲進度將遺失。

## 聯絡我們

若您透過電子郵件聯絡我們，我們會使用您提供的電子郵件地址及訊息內容，回覆您的問題或處理支援需求。

您主動提供的附件也可能包含在聯絡郵件中。這些郵件透過 Gmail 收發及儲存，涉及 Google 郵件服務的資料處理。

與《紙光》相關的聯絡郵件未設定固定保存期限；若您未提出刪除要求，郵件將持續保留。您可透過下方信箱要求刪除先前提供的聯絡郵件及附件。

聯絡信箱：[gamegamelife@gmail.com](mailto:gamegamelife@gmail.com)

## 資料安全

本機遊戲資料由您裝置上的儲存與安全機制保護；聯絡郵件則透過 Gmail 的帳戶與服務安全機制處理。任何電子儲存或傳輸方式皆無法保證絕對安全。

## 政策更新

本政策如有更新，將於此頁公布並更新生效日期。

# Privacy Policy for 《紙光》

Effective date: September 17, 2026

《紙光》 (package name: com.paperlight.puzzle) is a game that can be played entirely offline.

## Data handling

We do not collect or transmit players' personal data through this game. The game contains no advertisements and does not integrate user analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

The game does not offer in-app purchases, account sign-in, or cloud saves. It does not access photos, location, contacts, or similar personal data.

## Local data retention and deletion

Game progress and settings are stored only on your phone and are not uploaded by the game to the developer's servers.

You can delete local game progress and settings by clearing this app's storage in Android settings. Clearing this data will erase your game progress.

## Contact us

If you contact us by email, we use the email address and message content you provide to respond to your questions or handle your support request.

Contact emails may also include attachments you voluntarily provide. These emails are sent, received, and stored through Gmail and involve Google's processing of data as part of its email service.

We do not set a fixed retention period for emails relating to 《紙光》. Unless you request deletion, these emails will continue to be retained. You may use the address below to request deletion of previously provided contact emails and attachments.

Contact: [gamegamelife@gmail.com](mailto:gamegamelife@gmail.com)

## Data security

Local game data is protected by your device's storage and security mechanisms. Contact emails are handled through Gmail's account and service security mechanisms. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Any updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
